# Privacy policy

_The German version is authoritative. Last updated: 31 October 2026._

## In short

This policy applies to the Dance Home app on iPhone, iPad, Mac (with Apple silicon) and Android. The app processes your data on your device only, as a rule. There is no user account, no tracking, no advertising of our own and no analytics. Data leaves your device only if you expressly send a note, a video or a song via the share menu (through the service you choose there) or expressly turn on one of the optional features – sync via iCloud or Google Drive, sharing in dance circles or, in a future version, sharing your assessment of how danceable music is. These features are off by default. On Apple devices the app also passes a small state (module, dance, playback position) to your own devices via Handoff, see “Handoff between your Apple devices”. For TestFlight and Google Play tests see “Apple and Google services”.

## What data the app processes

The app processes the following data on your device:

- Videos (and, if you like, photos) and music you choose or share with the app are copied into the app's storage. The originals in your photo library, music library or files remain unchanged. On the Mac you can also drag files from the Finder into the app window; only the dragged files are copied.
- Tags, notes, ratings, your own dances and settings are stored in a database on your device.
- When importing, the app keeps the metadata stored in the file or library (for example time and place of recording and camera model) and stores it on your device.
- When you record a video in the app, it uses the camera and microphone; the recording is stored in the app's storage like other videos.
- Voice commands (Siri or Google Assistant) are processed by your device's operating system. The app only receives the recognized command, for example the name of a dance.

The legal basis for this processing is providing the features you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

## Permissions

The app only asks for access a feature needs, and only when you use that feature: Photos (import videos and photos), media library (import music and Apple Music), camera (record videos), microphone (sound for recordings) and, on Android, notifications (background playback). You can revoke any permission at any time in your device settings.

## Storage, backup and deletion

The data is stored in the app's storage on your device. It is part of your device backup (iCloud or Google backup, or a backup on your computer) if you have turned it on. In the app you can delete individual videos and songs (Settings › Storage). If you delete the app, all of its data is removed from the device.

## Sync via iCloud or Google Drive (optional)

Dance Home can sync your data between your devices. This feature is off by default and only becomes active if you turn it on yourself.

- **What is transferred:** your videos and music as well as tags, notes (on videos and on dances), ratings, your own dances and settings.
- **Where to:** exclusively to your own storage with Apple (iCloud) or Google (Google Drive). The respective provider's privacy terms apply to storage and transfer. The controller of Dance Home has no access to this data.
- **Turning it off:** at any time in the app's settings. You manage or delete data already transferred in your iCloud or Google account.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by turning the feature on and can withdraw at any time with effect for the future by turning it off.

## Dance circles (optional, iPhone, iPad and Mac)

You can create one dance circle of your own and invite up to five people via iCloud; you can join other people's dance circles when they invite you. New videos, music and notes on dances are private by default; only what you expressly share into your dance circle is shared via iCloud with its members, including title, tags and notes. Only the person who created a dance circle shares new items; members with “View and edit” can change titles, tags and notes, and their name is stored as the last change. The content of a dance circle is stored in the iCloud of the person who created it. You can make content private again at any time, delete your dance circle (its content then stays private with you) or leave someone else's dance circle.

- **Reporting and hiding:** If you report an entry in someone else's dance circle, it is hidden for you at once, and the report (which entry, the chosen reason, your text for “Other”, your name, the time) is stored in the dance circle. It is kept in the dance circle’s shared iCloud area; the app shows it only to the person who created the dance circle. They also see that you reported it and can remove you from the dance circle. If they do not decide within 5 hours, or if a second person reports the same entry, it is hidden as a precaution for all members until they decide. People and entries you hide for yourself are remembered on your device only. Reports are not sent to the controller of Dance Home.
- **Access:** The person who created a dance circle decides who may change titles, tags and notes (“View and edit” or “View only”) and can remove people. This information is also stored in the dance circle.

Sharing uses Apple's iCloud features; the controller of Dance Home has no access. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

## Sharing videos and music with others (on request)

With the share icon in the details of a video or song you can pass on a copy via your device's share menu (e.g. Messages, AirDrop, Mail, “Save to Files”). Only what you choose there is shared, with the recipients and via the service you choose. If you like, title, dance, tags and note (for music: dances, tags and tempo) are stored in the shared file – either as a Dance Home entry (a `.dancehome` file containing the copy and these details) or in the shared video or music file. The shared copy keeps all of the file’s metadata, including the recording location and camera model; a Dance Home entry additionally contains all details Dance Home has stored for the video or song (e.g. recording time, original file name and the link to the original in your library). For Apple Music songs only a link is shared. The app itself sends nothing to the controller or to third parties. You are responsible for what you share (e.g. copyright, personal image rights).

## Handoff between your Apple devices

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, Dance Home uses Apple's “Handoff” feature to offer continuing on another of your devices where you left off. For this the app hands the system only a small state: the open module, the active dance, the identifier of the video or songs, the playback position, the metronome setting (tempo, meter) and the scroll position of the list – no videos, no music files, no titles or notes. Apple transfers this state only to devices signed in with the same Apple Account that have Handoff turned on; it does not appear in search or in Siri suggestions. You can turn Handoff off in your device's settings (iPhone/iPad: General › AirPlay & Continuity; Mac: General › AirDrop & Handoff). The controller of Dance Home receives none of this.

## Assessing danceability (planned, optional)

In a future version you will optionally be able to assess how well a piece of music suits a particular dance. The assessments of all participants are combined into suggestions of which dances a piece of music fits; the app makes these suggestions available to all users.

- **What is transferred:** your assessment, the dance concerned and the details needed to identify the song (for example title, artist and tempo). The music file itself is not transferred.
- **Anonymous:** The transfer to the controller's server takes place without a user account and without your name, email address, device identifier or any other attribute that would allow conclusions about your person. Your device's IP address is processed only as technically required for the transfer, is not stored and is not linked to the assessments.
- **Voluntary:** The feature is off by default and can be turned off again at any time. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

This feature is not yet available. Before it is introduced, this policy will be supplemented with details on the server location, service providers used and the retention period.

## Apple and Google services

- **Apple Music:** If you turn on Apple Music in the app, the app queries your library and the catalog through Apple's MusicKit interface. Apple's privacy policy applies.
- **App Store, TestFlight and Google Play:** When you download and update the app, Apple or Google process data under their own privacy policies. If you test the app via TestFlight, the controller receives from Apple your name and email address from the invitation, crash reports and any feedback you send yourself. If you test the app via a Google Play test, the controller receives from Google your email address from the tester list, any feedback you send yourself and crash and performance data aggregated by Google (Android vitals). This data is used only to improve the app.

## No sharing

Your data is not sold and not used for advertising. Data is transferred only to the storage services you choose (iCloud, Google Drive), to the members of a dance circle you invite and – if you turn on assessing danceability – anonymously to the controller's server.

## Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time. Please contact the email address above. You can also lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (www.dsb.gv.at).

## Changes

If new features process data differently, this policy will be updated beforehand. The current version is available in the app under More › About & licenses › Privacy and on the web.
