# Privacy policy

_Draft (E30-S08) – to be legally reviewed before publication. The German version is authoritative. Last updated: 12 October 2026._

## In short

The Dance Home app processes your data on your device only, as a rule. There is no user account, no tracking, no advertising and no analytics. Data leaves your device only if you expressly turn on one of the optional features – sync via iCloud or Google Drive or, in a future version, sharing your assessment of how danceable music is. Both features are off by default. For TestFlight see “Apple and Google services”.

## What data the app processes

The app processes the following data on your device:

- Videos and music you choose are copied into the app's storage. The originals in your photo library, music library or files remain unchanged.
- Tags, notes, ratings, your own dances and settings are stored in a database on your device.
- The app uses the microphone only when you start listening through the microphone to recognize the dance and beat of the music playing. The audio is analyzed in memory only, never recorded and never sent.
- Voice commands (Siri or Google Assistant) are processed by your device's operating system. The app only receives the recognized command, for example the name of a dance.

The legal basis for this processing is providing the features you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

## Permissions

The app only asks for access a feature needs, and only when you use that feature: Photos (import videos), media library (import music and Apple Music), microphone (listening) and, on Android, notifications (background playback). You can revoke any permission at any time in your device settings.

## Storage, backup and deletion

The data is stored in the app's storage on your device. It is part of your device backup (iCloud or Google backup, or a backup on your computer) if you have turned it on. In the app you can delete individual videos and songs (Settings › Storage). If you delete the app, all of its data is removed from the device.

## Sync via iCloud or Google Drive (optional)

Dance Home can sync your data between your devices. This feature is off by default and only becomes active if you turn it on yourself.

- **What is transferred:** your videos and music as well as tags, notes, ratings, your own dances and settings.
- **Where to:** exclusively to your own storage with Apple (iCloud) or Google (Google Drive). The respective provider's privacy terms apply to storage and transfer. The controller of Dance Home has no access to this data.
- **Turning it off:** at any time in the app's settings. You manage or delete data already transferred in your iCloud or Google account.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by turning the feature on and can withdraw at any time with effect for the future by turning it off.

## Assessing danceability (planned, optional)

In a future version you will optionally be able to assess how well a piece of music suits a particular dance. The assessments of all participants are combined into suggestions of which dances a piece of music fits; the app makes these suggestions available to all users.

- **What is transferred:** your assessment, the dance concerned and the details needed to identify the song (for example title, artist and tempo). The music file itself is not transferred.
- **Anonymous:** The transfer to the controller's server takes place without a user account and without your name, email address, device identifier or any other attribute that would allow conclusions about your person. Your device's IP address is processed only as technically required for the transfer, is not stored and is not linked to the assessments.
- **Voluntary:** The feature is off by default and can be turned off again at any time. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

This feature is not yet available. Before it is introduced, this policy will be supplemented with details on the server location, service providers used and the retention period.

## Apple and Google services

- **Apple Music:** If you turn on Apple Music in the app, the app queries your library and the catalog through Apple's MusicKit interface. Apple's privacy policy applies.
- **App Store, TestFlight and Google Play:** When you download and update the app, Apple or Google process data under their own privacy policies. If you test the app via TestFlight, the controller receives from Apple your name and email address from the invitation, crash reports and any feedback you send yourself. This data is used only to improve the app.

## No sharing

Your data is not sold and not used for advertising. Data is transferred only to the storage services you choose (iCloud, Google Drive) and – if you turn on assessing danceability – anonymously to the controller's server.

## Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time. Please contact the email address above. You can also lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (www.dsb.gv.at).

## Changes

If new features process data differently, this policy will be updated beforehand. The current version is available in the app under More › About & licenses › Privacy and on the web.
